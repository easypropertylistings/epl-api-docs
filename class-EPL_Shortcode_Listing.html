Class EPL_Shortcode_Listing

[listing] on the [listing_advanced] engine

Same defaults, args filter (epl_shortcode_listing_args), sorting context and template as [listing] always had, plus every [listing_advanced] option: feature, feature_id, location_id, open_house, auction, commercial_listing_type, post__in, post__not_in, orderby_clause and wrap_template.
